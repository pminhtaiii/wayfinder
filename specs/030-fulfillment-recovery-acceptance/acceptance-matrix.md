## Gate and reporting

Relevant changes to payment/fulfillment adapters, recovery, persistence/migrations, queue/operator actions, checkout/payment UI, harness or CI require the controlled suite alongside focused U/I checks. Required job must execute nonzero expected scenario counts and fail when infrastructure is unhealthy, discovery is empty, required scenarios are skipped or its invocation is omitted; do not treat absence as success. Publish scenario counts and redacted failed-case evidence. Current CI integration tests and T093 chat system runner do not satisfy this feature gate.

Sandbox runs separately, manually or nightly with explicit test credentials and verified account capabilities. Missing credentials produce an explicit sandbox-skipped report, never a claim that sandbox validation passed or a substitute green controlled gate. Record provider API/SDK version, account test mode, supported fields and open limitations; credential availability must not block deterministic CI.

I/C assertions require one reserved workflow/payment operation, no fake provider IDs, no unsupported second intent, fenced cleanup and no supplier creation until authorization is verified.

Extend A17 with rollback during a held supplier request, later signed events, new HTTP retries and workers. Enrolled unresolved cases must never fall back to legacy compensation. Retain evidence/enrollment routing. A09's signed-event portion and A10 restart recovery depend on US2; US1 independently demonstrates customer flow and pending states without claiming operator/scheduled recovery is already complete.

Lane mapping: A01-A15, A17, A18 and A21 have controlled assertions; A16-A17 additionally use clean/upgrade DB fixtures; A19 is isolated sandbox; A20 is actual-timeout with >=180 seconds per test and an overall service budget exceeding the >=130-second threshold.
